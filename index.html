Before You Go

  • Government photo ID (driver's license or passport)
  • MBON / CJIS authorization details (ORI, codes or forms, if provided)
  • Payment: credit/debit plus backup card or cash
  • Appointment confirmations (screenshots and phone numbers)
  • Charged phone, charger or power bank
  • Call each shop Friday to confirm slot, MBON acceptance, price and payment type

Mission Rules

One accepted submission is the goal. Get a receipt or transaction number, cancel the remaining stops, and return to DCA. If a site no-shows or rejects paperwork, leave quickly and move to the next stop. Request Lyft 10–15 minutes before each departure.
